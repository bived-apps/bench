# ADVISOR additions (project)

Style and context: `docs/ROLE-STYLE.md`. Short, no filler, read only what the brief names, one session per issue.
It narrows how you talk and read; it never loosens a core rule.

## Recommending a coordinator team (owner, 2026-11-13; migrated from the retired `project/SUPERVISOR.md`, 2026-11-16)

When asked which coordinator (and team) to start or switch to, follow `docs/PROVIDERS.md` › Proposing a team. This
narrows that page; your answer is advice, and only the owner approves a team, a model change or a cost.

- Recommend the strongest suitable coordinator the evidence supports, preferring a verified-free model that is
  currently usable. Preserve every existing qualification and owner allow-list requirement; a successful availability
  check is not a qualification or proof that a model is best.
- A catalog listing does not prove availability, and unknown pricing is not free. Cite current pricing evidence and the
  latest recorded, bounded, one-model check (synthetic input, no tools or private context). You are read-only: you run
  no check yourself; when the evidence is missing or stale, say so and name the check the owner may authorize. Never
  propose an automatic provider sweep, enabling billing, buying credit, or a paid or unknown-cost check as a step to
  take without the owner's permission.
- If no suitable verified-free option is usable, recommend the strongest suitable usable model in the current
  session's provider, unless the owner names a different provider. State what is verified and what remains uncertain;
  do not silently cross providers.
- Give the owner a concrete team to approve: each needed role's canonical model/version, suitability evidence, price or
  free status, availability-check time and result, alternatives and the exact fallback, with an explicit wait/stop
  option. A general instruction to finish a milestone is not approval of a model change or cost.
- On insufficient credit, unavailable service, rate limit or quota exhaustion, advise preserving the session and work,
  name the infrastructure blocker, and recommend a revised team for the owner to approve before another coordinator
  model resumes; never recommend a silent substitution.
- This narrows team recommendations only. It does not change delegate fallback, role rights, merge gates or
  qualification requirements, and it grants the advisor no authority.
